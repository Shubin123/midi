Lesson texts in js/lessons.js

The lesson texts in js/lessons.js are adapted from Open Music Theory by Kris
Shaffer, Bryn Hughes, Brian Moseley and contributors, published by Hybrid
Pedagogy Publishing:

  https://openmusictheory.github.io
  https://github.com/openmusictheory/openmusictheory.github.io

Open Music Theory is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0). The texts
have been shortened and reworded for the keyboard, each lesson links the
chapter it comes from, and the adapted texts are shared under the same
license:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

This license covers only the lesson texts (the `text`, `task` and `hint`
strings of the LESSONS list). The rest of the project, including the code that
checks the tasks, is not covered by it.
